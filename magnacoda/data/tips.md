# *magnacoda* tips

- Not sure which options you need? Type `wizard`: it asks a few plain questions and builds the scan for you.
- Using a screen reader? Type `screenreader on`, so text arrives all at once and help prints instead of paging.
- Type `help`, then press g and type a section's number or name to go straight to it.
- In the help viewer, `/` searches all of it, not just the section showing.
- You can type just example.com or localhost:3000 as the page to scan: *magnacoda* fills in https:// or http:// (see the url-rules section of help).
- `docs` shows the whole manual, one section at a time; `docs scan` opens it at the scan options.
- `review` lists the scans you saved in this folder; pick one by number to review it in your browser, with its pictures and boxes to tick.
- `view FILE` shows any Markdown or text file, such as a scan's checklist.md, in the help viewer.
- Every scan checks dark mode too, in a few seconds, and the summary says which problems show up in only one of the two modes.
- Check the mobile layout too: add `--screen both` to scan a page at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Every scan is kept in `magnacoda-scans` in your Documents folder; `review` lists them.
- At the start of the wizard, type `recent` to pick one of your recent scans and change just the URL.
- At the end of the wizard you can save a scan as a favorite; `fav` lists your favorites.
- `news` shows updates, what's new in your version, and news about *magnacoda*. The banner also tells you when there's something new.
- `help` has an advanced section, with options for unusual pages: waiting longer, one theme only, an exact screen size.
- Don't want to be asked before a scan with the default settings? Set `confirm = off` under [scan] in preferences.ini.
- In help, press c to read it in other colors, like an old monitor's, or dark on light like print.
- Don't want these tips? Set `tips = off` under [display] in preferences.ini.
