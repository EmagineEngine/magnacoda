The code is under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, MIT Future License (FSL-1.1-MIT, see `LICENSE.md`), and each version becomes MIT-licensed two years after it's published, but neither license covers the name, only the code. This page explains how you may use the name, so people can always tell what comes from this project and what doesn't.

## You may

- Say truthfully that your work relates to *magnacoda*: "an add-on for *magnacoda*," "works with *magnacoda*," "a guide to *magnacoda*," "scanned with *magnacoda*."
- Name *magnacoda* in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews, comparisons and course material.
- Share unchanged copies of *magnacoda* under its own name.

## You may not

- Use "*magnacoda*" in the name of your own product, package, add-on, project, company, domain name or account. Give your add-on its own name, and describe it as "an add-on for *magnacoda*." For example, call it `example-reports` with the description "Extra report formats: an add-on for *magnacoda*," not `magnacoda-reports`.
- Use the words **pro**, **plus**, **premium**, **enterprise**, **official** or **team** in a way that suggests an edition or version of *magnacoda*, including in an add-on's name, description, or the edition name an add-on shows in *magnacoda*'s banner.
- Suggest that your work is made, endorsed, certified or supported by this project when it isn't.
- Use a *magnacoda* logo, or anything designed to look like one.
- Distribute a changed version of *magnacoda* under the name *magnacoda*. If you publish a modified copy (a fork), give it a different name; you may say it is "based on *magnacoda*."

## Please add a note

When you publish an add-on or other work built around *magnacoda*, include a line such as: "This is an independent add-on for *magnacoda*. It is not made or endorsed by the *magnacoda* project."

## Questions

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, or want permission for something not covered here, ask through the project's issue tracker: https://github.com/emagineengine/magnacoda/issues

## About the name

*magna* is Latin for "great," and a *coda* is the passage that brings a piece of music to its close. Accessibility is part of what makes code finished, and great: *magnacoda*.
